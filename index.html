Burn Rate — Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026 · Applies to Burn Rate for iPhone and Apple Watch

Burn Rate is an interval timer. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no server. We do not collect, receive, or transmit any personal data.

What stays on your devices

  • Plans and workout history. Stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and synchronised between them over Apple's device-to-device connection. They leave your devices only if you choose to export or share them.
  • Your name. Optional. Used to draw your avatar initials and for nothing else. You can change or remove it in Settings.
  • Settings. Cue, music, and display preferences.

Health data

  • On iPhone, Burn Rate writes a workout to Apple Health only if you turn on Save to Health, and asks for your permission at that moment. It does not read Health data on iPhone.
  • On Apple Watch, with Workout Mode on, Burn Rate reads your heart rate during a workout to display it and to record the heart-rate change after each BURN. Heart rate is shown as recorded and never interpreted, and the workout is saved to Health.
  • Health data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app.

Music

If you choose a song from your music library as BURN music, Burn Rate plays that song during hard intervals. It does not read or upload your library. If you pick an audio file, a private copy is kept inside the app.

Sharing

Sharing a plan creates a link or file containing only that plan's structure (names and durations). Exporting history creates a file on your device that you control. Nothing is shared unless you share it.

Tracking and third parties

Burn Rate does not track you, does not use third-party SDKs, and does not contain advertising.

Children

Burn Rate is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions, corrections, and privacy requests: open an issue in the Burn Rate support tracker. A GitHub account is free; no personal details are needed to post.
